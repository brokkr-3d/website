For his solo exhibition *Once Upon a Time* at Fineart on Tjuvholmen, Johnny Hurts
made a series of large sculptures and reliefs, some three to four metres across.
Few people have room for work at that scale.

So we made miniatures. In the way a lithograph is a smaller version of a large
painting, we produced three-dimensional lithographs of his sculptures —
reduced in scale, signed, and available to buy. The originals remain unique
pieces; the miniatures let more people take part of the show home.
