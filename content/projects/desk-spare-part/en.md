A customer came to us with a common problem: a screw in his desk had failed,
because of a small nylon coupling that was impossible to find anywhere. Too many
people answer that by buying a new desk.

We helped redesign the part and printed it in polycarbonate — strong and
durable. The result was a fully working desk with a sturdy, long-lasting
replacement. Set against the material cost of a whole new desk, it is strange this
isn't the default.
