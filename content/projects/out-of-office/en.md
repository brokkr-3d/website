Uppercase is a business-development studio at Skøyen that runs the annual Out of
Office Exhibition, inviting Norwegian artists to make work shown on large screens
at Aker Brygge and around the city. For this year's edition they needed an
illuminated sign.

We built a bright red sign from their logo, with varying letter depths for volume
and movement. The lighting runs on an Arduino and NeoPixels — each diode
addressable — with a simple program that gives the sign a faint flicker, like a
neon sign losing its connection. The same setup leaves room for other animations
later.
