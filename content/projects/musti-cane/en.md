Musti — Ugbad Mustafa Yusuf Noor, Norwegian rapper and singer — needed a finishing
touch for her performance at the P3 Gull music awards: a Black Panther-themed
staff to complete a gangster look.

We designed and produced the piece for the stage. Musti won Newcomer of the Year
at P3 Gull that year for her debut album *Qoyskayga* and the EP *Mellom
høyblokkene*.
