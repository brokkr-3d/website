For an event hosted by NBX (Norwegian Block Exchange) and Scandinavian Creative
Community, we made drink vouchers that were more than a paper slip — a small
physical keepsake from the night.

The tokens are round, about four to four and a half centimetres, carrying the NBX
and SCC logos in their own colours. Guests got three each on arrival. Many chose
to take them home rather than redeem them: fewer drinks at the bar, far more
people leaving with a souvenir.
