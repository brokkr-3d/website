Pearl Octopuss.Y wanted store fixtures that broke from the generic and carried
the brand's own energy — pieces that could show its identity while staying true
to its philosophy of exploring boundaries and mirroring a surreal, unstable world
while offering an escape from it.

Together we developed an 80-centimetre 3D-printed conch with a concealed mounting
system, made to be easy to install yet elegant and sculptural on its own. Printed
in PLA and stainless steel, produced in Oslo. It has since become a signature
element of Pearl Octopuss.Y's visual identity, still used in pop-up stores and
exhibitions including Oslo Airport.
