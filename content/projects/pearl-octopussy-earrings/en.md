Katrine, who founds the jewellery brand Pearl Octopuss.Y, works intuitively and
with a strong sense of form but no digital modelling. So she sculpted the
earrings in clay.

We 3D-scanned the models, prepared them for printing, and produced them in a
lightweight material — the point being to make large earrings that stay light. The
target was under fifteen grams, and we reached roughly eight-centimetre earrings
within it. For a second set we wrote a custom toolpath that lays material down in
a partly randomised structure, giving organic spiral forms unique to the brand.
