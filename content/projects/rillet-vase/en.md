Rillet ("ribbed" in Norwegian) is a vase drawn specifically for the way a 3D
printer builds: a continuous vertical rhythm of ridges that turns the layer
lines of the process into the object's surface language instead of hiding them.

The form is printed in a recycled polymer and produced in small, repeatable
runs. Each colourway — here a deep blue and a warm coral — is a short edition
rather than a mass product, so the tooling cost stays at zero and the design can
keep evolving between batches.
