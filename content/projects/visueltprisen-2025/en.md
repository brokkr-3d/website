Every year Norway's designers gather for Visueltprisen, the awards for the best
in the field. When the organisers wanted to renew the trophy, they asked
Norwegian Trash and Brokkr to explore how plastic waste could become a symbol of
creativity and sustainability. The old trophy was clear acrylic; the new one is
recycled plastic, melted and pressed together.

The material is honest. It doesn't try to imitate virgin plastic — its texture,
colour and history are all on show. The result is a trophy that carries both
craft and change, and a reminder that good design can be circular.
