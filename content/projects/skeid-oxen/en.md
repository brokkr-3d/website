We have built several parts of Skeid's physical branding: winner trophies for
their tournaments, and awards for Player of the Month, Player of the Year and Top
Scorer of the Month. What holds the set together is the bull — a central symbol
for Skeid, rooted in the well-known bull statue at Torshov.

We designed a 2025 version of the bull that runs through the whole series. A
smaller variant goes to new companies joining Skeid's business network — a
tangible sign that they are part of the club.
