Prilla Generalen is a stage act from an artist known for Swedish afterski and
party songs. The character needed a mask to finish the look.

We designed it with a form language to match the character and made it
comfortable to wear, fitting black lenses cut from a pair of sunglasses. The
build was adapted so he can sing without trouble and clips securely to the hat he
wears on stage.
