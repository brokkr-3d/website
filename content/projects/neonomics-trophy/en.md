Neonomics runs an annual table-tennis tournament and needed a challenge trophy to
carry the tradition. At the office there is a figure called Manesh, known for his
rather extreme serving stance — he became the trophy: *The Golden Manesh*.

We already had a 3D scan of the figure, converted it for printing, and handled
the whole production from print and assembly to gold lacquering — a distinctive,
funny trophy that fits both the tournament and the culture at Neonomics.
