Alveare is a series of seven lamps designed with Ingrid Aspen. Each one is built
from spherical volumes that meet and push into each other, creating concave and
convex surfaces and a strongly sculptural, almost restless form.

The shapes were too complex for standard slicing. We wrote our own toolpath
software so the print head could vary layer height within a single print and move
in a more organic path, which gave the surface an almost glass-like quality. It
is the kind of job — precise, technically new, visually specific — that Brokkr is
built for.
