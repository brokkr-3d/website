Haglaga released a summer song built around a character called Johnny Skinnvest,
and wanted a full stage costume with a fully covering mask — a marshmallow shape
with beer caps for eyes, based on their own AI renderings.

We took the images and built the whole mask, eyes included, in a form that looked
right and sat comfortably on the head. It was made so the performer could jump
and dance without it shifting; the mouth is a black felt-covered frame so he can
still sing clearly with the large head on. The character ran through the whole
summer tour.
