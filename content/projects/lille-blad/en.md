Lille Blad makes a hydroponic system for growers who want to farm vegetables at
small scale — easy to install, used to grow lettuce and microgreens that go to
restaurants in Oslo through Lille Blad's network.

As they scaled up, investing in moulds for the system's parts wasn't yet viable,
so they came to us for something more flexible. We produced more than a thousand
components, each one watertight and strong enough for continuous use. It let them
reach the market faster, at much lower cost, without giving up quality.
