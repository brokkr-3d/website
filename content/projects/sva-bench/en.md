SVA is a bench where natural material and recycled plastic come together in one
honest expression. The material can be reused up to thirty times, so circularity
is built into the design rather than added afterwards.

Its soft sand colour and tactile surface take after the polished stones along the
Norwegian coast — shaped by ice, water and time. SVA carries that slow natural
process into a contemporary object: material knowledge, restrained form, and a
clear respect for resources. Made to last, and made to return.
