Mitt Spor is a subscription service from DyreID and Telenor that tracks your dog
live from your phone via a GPS collar tag. The original tag was too fragile and
not intuitive enough to use. With Live Aamo and Sasha Milic we set out to make a
cover that was simple to handle but tough enough to survive a 40-kilo dog
crashing through the undergrowth.

A workshop set the goals; we prototyped three versions for internal testing,
narrowed to one, refined it, and sent it out for field testing and a small
webshop run to gather customer feedback. Then it launched. Production is local —
so far more than 5,000 covers made here in Oslo.
