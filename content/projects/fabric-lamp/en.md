Ingrid Aspen wanted a lamp that looked like a piece of fabric hanging from its
cord. The problem is that fabric left to hang freely collapses and loses its
shape and volume.

We built an internal structure in two-millimetre PET that supports the textile
and shapes it into soft folds. The construction stays hidden, so the fabric keeps
its weight and expression without losing its form. Ingrid Aspen and Ateljé Lyktan
developed the idea further for the Oslo Design Fair, and the lamp was featured in
*Vi bor bedre*.
