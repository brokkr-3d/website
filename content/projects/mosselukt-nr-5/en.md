Yamile Calderón came to us with *Mosselukta Nr. 5*, an installation in Moss that
plays Chanel No. 5 against the town's own well-known smell. She needed an icon for
it: a large perfume bottle.

We designed the bottle in a clear red material, prepared the print files, and
developed the box it sits in, produced with Copycat. The finished bottle stands
over a metre tall, branded *Mosselukt Nr. 5* — a playful stack of references that
became a distinctive, funny art object.
