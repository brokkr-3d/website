Biophilic Superstructures is a series of 3D-printed artworks made for TAD Oslo and
designed by Adrian Brun. Their organic shapes and tactile surface play on the
human pull towards nature.

We produced four pieces in wood fibre and recycled plastic — corn-derived
bioplastic (PLA) that can be composted at end of life. The forms shift the feel
of the room, giving the mind somewhere to settle.
