We have worked with Lighthouse Company on several pieces, including their modular
Lab lamp series, testing shapes and shades together.

The first job was an assembly problem on the Jedi Snake lamp — a light tube that
coils around a steel pipe, part lightsaber, part snake. They had no clean way to
fix the tube to the pipe. We developed a bracket that locks the two together and
gives the lamp a finished look, printed in polycarbonate after a run of tests to
settle the locking mechanism. Lighthouse Company now uses the same bracket as
standard.
