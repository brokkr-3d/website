Fjuz is a marketing and communications agency. To mark a new access road to Oslo
Airport they wanted a physical exhibition at Gardermoen that would catch travellers'
attention while they waited for their luggage.

The centrepiece was an interactive racetrack, with a miniature city built around
it — Oslo landmarks and a Flytoget train model tying it to the airport. We
designed and produced a set of the components, including a detailed lighthouse
and several colourful elements that pulled the installation together.
