For their next tour, Undergrunn came back with another brief: custom shoulder pads
for one of their stage outfits. After an exploratory design process we drew on one
of the oldest known Norwegian design motifs — the wood carving at Urnes Stave
Church, more than a thousand years old and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

We rebuilt the motif in a contemporary form and used it as the base for the
handcrafted pads. In keeping with the album *Norge elsker rap*, the result puts
ancient carving on the artists as they perform for a young, modern crowd. Worn on
stage at the Findings festival.
