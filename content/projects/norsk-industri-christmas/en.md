Norsk Industri is an employers' organisation that champions Norwegian
manufacturing. For a staff Christmas gift they wanted something as local as
possible, well made and carefully done.

We designed an egg-shaped container that opens, hangs on the tree and holds
chocolate — the chocolate from Heimdal Chokolade in Arendal, a small producer
with the same bias towards quality and local roots. We also made a set of office
decorations built from the Norsk Industri logo, reworking its geometry into
something recognisable but new, with an assembly that is satisfying to put
together by hand.
