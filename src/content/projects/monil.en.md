Monil makes electronic livestock collars. Each collar carries GPS and a small
power source and creates a virtual fence: as an animal nears the boundary it gets
gradually stronger signals and learns to stay inside.

Producing the first units, Monil needed to strengthen the physical design for
durability. We developed and produced several thousand custom clips that improved
the structure and the long-term reliability of the devices.
