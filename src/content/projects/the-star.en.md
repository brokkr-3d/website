Trym Ruud is a digital artist who has spent years creating and selling 3D art. We
have worked with him since 2020–2021 on bringing that digital universe into
physical form, through a run of pieces that grew from small artworks to
large-scale installations.

The Star is the most ambitious of them: the largest 3D-printed humanoid statue
made to date. It is printed in PLA, a bio-based plastic, on a stainless-steel
structure, and produced in Oslo.
