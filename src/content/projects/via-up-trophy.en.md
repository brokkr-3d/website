VIA VIKA in Oslo has one of the city's most distinctive façades. For their annual
staircase run, VIA UP, they wanted a trophy that carried the building's character.

We designed it as a double-helix staircase that ends in the VIA UP logo, standing
on a base that picks up details from the complex itself — the tilted round window,
the characteristic joints between the elements. Printed in PLA, made in Oslo.
