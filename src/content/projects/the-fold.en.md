The Fold is a sculptural lamp inspired by a tulip and made entirely from recycled
material. Its form is a double-walled surface that only 3D printing can produce,
which gives it a soft, even light. The print lines are hand-sculpted into the
form, printed with our own patented process.

At roughly 35 cm tall it is light and simple to install. It comes both as a
consumer version with Philips Hue and in a version for commercial projects.
