VOFFii is a Norwegian app for dog owners, trainers and anyone thinking about
getting a dog. To give a campaign a physical side, we developed a concept that
bridged the digital and the tangible.

The result was a series of 3D-printed dog figures on small bases, used in an
interactive treasure hunt where people could find the figures and take them home.
Two smaller statues were made for the *Fido — Seek!* part of the campaign.
