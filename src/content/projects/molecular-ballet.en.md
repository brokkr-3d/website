The Molecular Ballet is an art project that pulls music, geometry, philosophy and
history into one universe, with characters built from geometric shapes and
symbolism.

We 3D-printed the characters so the performance could be set out as a miniature,
and made models of the places they appear — among them the Arctic Cathedral, the
Solar Observatory and a farm-like arena. Together the figures and scenes form a
physical display of the whole world they have made.
