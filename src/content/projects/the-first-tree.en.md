Øyvind Engvik — Engwin — is a Norwegian artist known for digital work with a
strong narrative pull. One of his series imagines an apocalyptic future where
Earth has collapsed and humanity has moved to Mars; *The First Tree* is its
keystone, the first tree planted in a new world.

We took it from digital file to a physical sculpture around two and a half metres
tall — working out how it should be built to stand stable, how to split it into
printable components, and printing the whole thing. It took a lot of filament to
reach the form Engwin wanted. The finished piece has a strong presence and was
covered in Dagens Næringsliv and elsewhere.
