Blåne is a cabin maker who wanted customers to be able to see and hold their
cabins as physical models. We made both large display models, around 60
centimetres, and smaller miniatures for prospective buyers to take home.

The models are built with a high level of detail and colours matched to the real
materials — the tone of the timber cladding, the green grass roof, the
characteristic black accents — so the quality Blåne stands for comes across in
the hand.
