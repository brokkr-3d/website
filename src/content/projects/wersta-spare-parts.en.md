Werksta is a car-repair chain across Sweden, Denmark, Norway, Finland and the
Netherlands, working to be more sustainable while delivering faster.

As a proof of concept we produced a cover for a Citroën C4 Picasso — a
high-wear part — 3D-printed in carbon-fibre-reinforced polycarbonate, with
strength close to the original, plus a gear shifter for a Peugeot by the same
method. The advantage over sourcing originals is time and cost: two days instead
of two weeks, made in Norway, on demand, with no inventory and less material and
transport per part.
