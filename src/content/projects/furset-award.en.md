Fursetgruppen runs several cafeterias and wanted to recognise the teams that
delivered best over the year — with an award that was simple, clear and read as a
mark of quality.

We designed a gold bar with the logo and the winner's name, its form clean and
solid, the brand colour worked into the object. A plain but distinctive thing
that gives the winner something tangible and stands out in a cafeteria.
