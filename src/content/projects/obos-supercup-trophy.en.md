Skeid runs the OBOS Supercup, an invitational pre-season tournament for 2nd and
3rd division teams. They wanted a trophy, a challenge cup and a Player of the Year
award, all built from the cup's logo.

We took the logo into three dimensions: a gold-and-white ball set against
elements made from recycled fishing nets — a material that adds texture and a
clear link to Norwegian coastal life.
