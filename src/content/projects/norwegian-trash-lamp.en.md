The Norwegian Trash Lamp is a small study in what waste plastic can become. The
body is printed from recycled material, with the print process left visible so
the origin of the plastic is part of the object rather than something to
disguise.

It is a self-initiated piece — a way to test recycled feedstock, surface quality
and diffusion on something we would actually want on a desk.
