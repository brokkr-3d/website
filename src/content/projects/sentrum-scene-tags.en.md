Sentrum Scene needed clear, durable numbering for more than a thousand seats. The
first idea was plastic clips with stickers; we saw a better one.

Instead we designed custom clips in two colours with the numbers built into the
form, so they read in low light and can be found by touch. The result is a solid,
lasting product that pulls the seating rows together and lifts the overall sense
of quality in the venue.
