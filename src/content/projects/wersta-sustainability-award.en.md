The workshop chain wanted a dedicated sustainability award, made from a material
that genuinely reflected their values. They already had a trophy tradition; this
one had to say something.

We took the existing trophy, reworked it to suit 3D printing, and produced it in
recycled ocean plastic — rope from the Norwegian fishing industry that would
otherwise be waste, turned into a printable granulate. The result is a green,
distinctive trophy that carries a story matching the award.
