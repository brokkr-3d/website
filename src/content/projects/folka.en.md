We had wanted to work with the artist Nix for a long time. The chance came with
Folka, an exhibition that needed something large, physical and visually striking.
Nix — Nikolai Gyllenhammar, half of the Oslo duo BROSLO — had made a series of VR
sketches for wall-mounted pieces that sit between painting and sculpture, roughly
three by two metres.

The file was far too large to print in one piece, so we split it into modules and
reworked the design for seamless wall mounting. Printing it brought the weight
down to around 50 kilograms instead of several hundred while keeping the
monumental scale. We deliberately increased the layer height in production to
give the surface a coarse, hand-made, unmistakably printed texture — so the work
quite literally emerges from the wall.
