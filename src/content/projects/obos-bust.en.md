OBOS came to us when their Communications Director was retiring after many years,
and wanted to give him a bust. Scanning someone without their knowledge is hard,
so we worked with Jannicke, who filmed him in a natural situation.

From the footage we generated a first 3D mesh with an AI tool, then spent a few
hours cleaning and remodelling it so the proportions and expression were right.
We designed a simple plaque for the base and printed the bust in its final form —
a personal, solid marker for the end of a long career.
